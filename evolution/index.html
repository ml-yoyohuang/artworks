      How to read a specimen
      
  
      
    
      Body scale
      size gene; displayed radius is enlarged ×1.5. Shape also changes area; it is not a physical footprint.
      
    
      Elongation, tail length
      forager maximum speed gene; other types use their own inherited silhouettes
      
    
      Tail beat
      how fast it is moving now
      
    
      Internal structures
      grazer rays run from the energy core toward the inside of the membrane. Generalist lobes have 0–3 inherited decorative particles each; they do not add genes or capabilities.
      
    
      Antennae
      only the forager has external antennae. Selected dotted rays show the actual three sensor directions for every type; internal details are visual metaphors.
      
    
      Side notch
      forager's share of energy given to each offspring
      
    
      Outline ripples
      forager controller genes, used decoratively; no modeled membrane adaptation
      
    
      Form and colour family
      orange–blue oval = forager; blue–green polygon = grazer; pink–orange lobes = generalist. Within-family tint uses two neutral inherited markers.
      
    
      Corners / lobes
      inherited integer traits, changed only at birth; affect uptake, maintenance and construction. More is not always better.
      
    
      Dark nucleus
      energy reserve metaphor, not a simulated organ
      
    
      Water, sediment
      blue = A, green tint = B. Smoothed decorative maps; motes are not countable food items. Read availability in field notes.
      
    
      Division, fading
      observed births and deaths, shown briefly at low speeds. Model 2 dissipates corpse reserves; legacy model 1 returns them to A.
      
  
      
  
      Organisms move using inherited controllers. Births require energy and maturity; deaths arise from starvation or age-dependent random hazard. Three types are designed starting categories, not observed speciation. Diversity is mean raw gene variance, not heterozygosity. Birth/death rates cover the latest recorded interval; lineage and trait statistics use the latest recorded sample. Save a snapshot to preserve interventions; the seed alone reproduces only the initial experiment. Hidden tabs suspend simulation time without catching up. At high speed the frame budget may lower actual tick rate; every executed tick uses the same scientific rules. See docs/STAGE3_REPORT.md for measured evidence.
