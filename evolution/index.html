    How to read a specimen

    Body scale
    size gene; displayed radius is enlarged ×1.5. Shape also changes area; it is not a physical footprint.
    Elongation, tail length
    maximum speed gene
    Tail beat
    how fast it is moving now
    Antennae
    visual metaphor for three sensor samples; length is compressed. Selected dotted rays show true range and directions.
    Side notch
    share of energy given to each offspring
    Outline ripples
    inherited controller genes, used decoratively; no modeled membrane adaptation
    Blue / orange tint
    two neutral marker genes: lineage colour, no effect on survival
    Dark nucleus
    energy reserve metaphor, not a simulated organ
    Water, sediment
    smoothed food and fertility maps. Decorative shoreline drift is not resource transport; motes are not countable food items.
    Division, fading
    observed births and deaths, shown briefly at low speeds. No nutrient recycling is simulated.

    Organisms follow inherited controllers. Births require energy, maturity and a controller output; deaths arise from starvation or age-dependent random hazard. Founder colours are not species. Diversity is mean raw gene variance, not heterozygosity. Birth/death rates cover the latest recorded interval; lineage and trait statistics use the latest recorded sample. Save a snapshot to preserve interventions; the seed alone reproduces only the initial experiment. Hidden tabs suspend simulation time without catching up.
